Foreign currency member pickup

Branch team members | Procedure | Effective September 10, 2026

Purpose

Provide a consistent, secure process for handing over foreign currency orders to members.

Sections (TOC)

This procedure contains 4 sections.

  • Basic Knowledge
  • Verify the member and order
  • Complete the pickup
  • Supporting Resources

Basic Knowledge

When to use

  • A member arrives at the branch to collect their foreign currency order.

Verify the member and order

Confirm the order is ready and the member is authorized to collect it.

  1. Ask the member for a valid government-issued photo ID.

  2. Locate the order in the currency ordering system.

  3. Match the member’s details to the order confirmation.

Important! Keep the order reference available throughout the pickup process.
IfThen

The member’s details do not match the order

Pause the pickup and contact the branch manager.

Complete the pickup

  1. Count the currency with the member.

  2. Record the collection and provide a receipt.

Warning! Do not release currency until identity verification is complete.

Expected result

The member receives their order and the collection is recorded.
